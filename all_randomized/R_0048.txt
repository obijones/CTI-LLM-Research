**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The primary attack vectors identified involve leveraging legitimate business communication and support tools. This allows them to establish a foothold within the network, escalate privileges, and ultimately deploy ransomware. The scope of this threat extends across multiple potential areas, including access to sensitive data, disruption of critical systems, and potential for lateral movement within the organization. Emphasis should be placed on verifying requests for remote assistance and reporting suspicious activity.
*   **Continuous Threat Monitoring:** Implement enhanced monitoring of our network and systems to detect suspicious activity, particularly related to Teams communications and remote access attempts.
