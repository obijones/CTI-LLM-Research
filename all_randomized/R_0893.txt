**1. Executive Summary**

A major cyberattack targeting Change Healthcare has exposed the sensitive personal and health information of potentially one in three Americans, creating a substantial and immediate risk to financial stability and operational continuity across the U.S. healthcare system. This attack, perpetrated by a ransomware group, represents a critical vulnerability within a foundational component of the nation’s healthcare infrastructure, demanding immediate and decisive action.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

This incident poses a severe threat to the financial health of numerous organizations reliant on Change Healthcare’s systems, potentially leading to significant operational disruptions, substantial financial losses due to remediation costs, and substantial reputational damage. Furthermore, the potential for widespread identity theft and fraud, coupled with the exposure of protected health information, creates a heightened risk of regulatory scrutiny and potential legal liabilities.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The cyberattack has compromised Change Healthcare’s systems, which process data for a vast number of healthcare providers and patients. This disruption impacts critical business processes including patient scheduling, claims processing, and medication management, directly affecting the ability of healthcare providers to deliver care and patients to access necessary services. The scale of the breach—potentially affecting one in three Americans—represents a systemic risk to the entire healthcare ecosystem.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Immediately Enhance Monitoring:** Implement enhanced monitoring of all patient data processing systems to detect and respond to any unauthorized access or data exfiltration, minimizing the potential for further data compromise.
*   **Implement Data Loss Prevention Controls:** Deploy robust data loss prevention (DLP) controls across all systems handling patient data to prevent sensitive information from leaving the organization’s control, reducing the risk of further data breaches.
*   **Strengthen Vendor Oversight:** Conduct a thorough review of Change Healthcare’s security practices and contractual obligations to ensure adequate protection of sensitive data and establish clear accountability for data breaches.
*   **Activate Business Continuity Plans:** Initiate and test business continuity plans designed to mitigate operational disruptions resulting from the cyberattack, ensuring continued access to patient care and financial services.
*   **Communicate Transparently:** Establish a clear and proactive communication strategy to inform patients, healthcare providers, and regulatory agencies about the incident and the steps being taken to address the situation, safeguarding the organization’s reputation.
