They are now involved in ransomware operations and leveraging access to Business Process Outsourcers (BPOs), significantly increasing the potential impact of a successful attack.

3. **Strengthen Identity Threat Detection & Response (ITDR):**  Deploy and fully leverage solutions like Cortex XDR and Cortex XSIAM to proactively monitor for anomalous user behavior and potential insider threats.

5. **Credential Hygiene:** Implement strict controls around user credentials, limiting access to only what is necessary and enforcing regular password resets.
6. **Continuous Monitoring & Threat Hunting:** Increase our proactive threat hunting efforts, focusing on identifying potential indicators of compromise (IOCs) associated with Muddled Libra.
