**1. Executive Summary**

LockBit 3.0 ransomware affiliates are actively targeting organizations across multiple critical infrastructure sectors, including financial services, posing a significant and immediate threat to our operations. This activity represents a serious risk of operational disruption, potential financial loss, and damage to our reputation. We must take immediate action to protect our systems and data.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

A successful LockBit 3.0 attack could result in substantial operational downtime, halting critical financial transactions and impacting our ability to serve customers. The disruption could lead to significant revenue losses, estimated at potentially millions of dollars, and could trigger substantial fines and penalties related to regulatory compliance failures. Furthermore, a public disclosure of a ransomware attack would severely damage our brand reputation and erode customer trust.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The threat actor’s activity is focused on Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software, which are integral to the delivery of our services. Compromise of these systems could disrupt access to our core banking platforms, impacting all business functions that rely on them, including account management, transaction processing, and customer service. This represents a broad exposure across our entire organization.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Immediately implement enhanced monitoring:** Increase the level of scrutiny applied to all systems utilizing Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software to detect any signs of compromise or malicious activity. This will allow us to quickly identify and respond to an attack in progress.
*   **Reimage compromised hosts:** If any potential compromise is suspected, immediately reimage all affected hosts to eliminate any malicious code or persistent access points established by the threat actor.
*   **Create new account credentials:** Following any suspected compromise, establish new, secure account credentials for all administrative accounts to prevent the threat actor from leveraging existing privileged access.
*   **Report the compromise:** Promptly report the suspected compromise to the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) and CISA via their Incident Reporting System to leverage their expertise and resources in investigating and mitigating the threat. State, local, tribal, or territorial government (SLTT) entities can also report to MS-ISAC.
*   **Assess Citrix software:** Conduct a thorough assessment of all Citrix software deployed across the organization to identify and remediate any vulnerabilities that could be exploited by the threat actor.
