The initial intelligence suggests a widespread impact, primarily targeting organizations utilizing SonicWall seventh-generation and newer firewalls. The vulnerability appears to be exacerbated by improperly migrated configurations from older firewall models, particularly concerning user account passwords. The scope includes all systems connected to networks utilizing these vulnerable firewalls.

**4. Recommended Actions**

To address this immediate threat, we recommend the following actions:

*   **Credential Rotation:** Immediately reset all local user account passwords for accounts with SSL VPN access, and rotate credentials for Active Directory integration.
