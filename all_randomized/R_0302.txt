* **Insider Threat Amplification:** The group’s reliance on insider access highlights the critical importance of robust access controls, monitoring, and employee training.
* **Detection & Prevention Recommendations (Immediate Action Required):**

    * **User Training & Vishing Awareness:** Conduct regular phishing simulations, specifically focusing on voice-based social engineering scenarios. Train employees to verify IT support calls through established channels.
    * **Anomaly Detection:** Deploy tools like Salesforce Shield, Okta ThreatInsight, and Microsoft security logs to identify unusual activity.
    * **Restrict Access:** Enforce the principle of least privilege, limiting employee access to sensitive data on a need-to-know basis.
