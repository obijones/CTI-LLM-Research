**Cyber Threat Intelligence Report: Polyfill.io Supply Chain Attack – Executive Summary**

1.  **Executive Summary**

We’ve identified a significant and rapidly evolving cyber threat stemming from a vulnerability within a widely used third-party service – polyfill.io. This service provides compatibility layers for web applications, allowing them to function correctly across a range of older browsers. However, a malicious actor exploited a weakness in this service to inject unauthorized code into numerous websites, impacting our customers’ online operations. This incident highlights the critical importance of carefully vetting and monitoring third-party services and the potential for supply chain attacks to severely disrupt business operations. The situation is dynamic, and we are actively working to contain the damage and prevent further exploitation.

2.  **Potential Business Impact**

The Polyfill.io attack represents a serious risk to our customers’ brand reputation, operational continuity, and overall security posture. The unauthorized code injected into websites could have led to data compromise, disrupted website functionality, and eroded user trust. The scale of the impact – affecting over 40 customers with potentially tens of thousands of impacted pages daily – demonstrates the potential for significant financial losses, including remediation costs, lost revenue due to downtime, and potential legal liabilities. Furthermore, this incident underscores the vulnerability of relying on third-party services and the need for robust supply chain risk management.

3.  **Affected Systems / Scope**

The attack exploited vulnerabilities within polyfill.io, a service used by a broad range of websites and web applications. The compromised code was inserted into these applications through a supply chain attack, meaning the risk extended beyond our direct control. Specifically, the attack targeted websites relying on polyfill.io for browser compatibility, impacting a diverse range of industries and customer segments. The scope includes websites utilizing the service for functionality across various browsers, creating a widespread vulnerability. The attack demonstrated the ability of a seemingly innocuous service to be leveraged for malicious purposes, highlighting the need for continuous monitoring of all third-party integrations.

Immediate action is required to mitigate the ongoing risk. We are taking the following steps:

*   **Enhanced Monitoring:** We are intensifying our real-time monitoring of websites utilizing polyfill.io, focusing on detecting and blocking any further malicious code injection.

*   **Policy Enforcement:** We are reinforcing our Client-Side Protection & Compliance policies to proactively defend against similar supply chain attacks.
