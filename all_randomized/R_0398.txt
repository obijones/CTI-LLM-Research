**Executive Threat Intelligence Report: Polyfill.io Supply Chain Attack**

**To:** Senior Business Leadership
**From:** [Your Name/Cybersecurity Analyst Team]
**Date:** October 26, 2023
**Subject:** Urgent: Critical Vulnerability in Third-Party JavaScript Dependencies – Mitigating Supply Chain Risk

**Executive Summary:**

We’ve identified a significant and active cyber threat stemming from a sophisticated supply chain attack involving the polyfill.io service. This attack exploited vulnerabilities in web applications relying on polyfills – JavaScript code that provides compatibility for older browsers – and resulted in the compromise of over 100,000 websites and potentially exposed our customers to significant risk.  The key takeaway is the heightened vulnerability of relying on third-party JavaScript libraries and the critical need for robust supply chain security measures.

**Key Findings:**

* **The Attack:** The attack leveraged compromised polyfill.io resources injected into web applications. This allowed attackers to execute malicious JavaScript code, including data theft (beacons) and potentially other harmful actions.
* **Scale of Impact:**  Over 40 Akamai customers were affected, with some experiencing tens of thousands of impacted pages daily. The attack targeted specific sessions based on user-agent filtering, highlighting the effectiveness of targeted attacks.
* **Root Cause:** The vulnerability lies in the unchecked inclusion of third-party JavaScript libraries, particularly polyfills, within web applications. This creates an entry point for attackers to inject malicious code.
* **Akamai’s Response:** Akamai’s Client-Side Protection & Compliance solution played a crucial role in detecting and mitigating the attack in real-time.  The solution’s in-browser detection and immediate mitigation capabilities prevented the malicious JavaScript from executing, stopping the attack before widespread damage occurred.  We were able to identify and block the compromised resources within seconds.
* **Attack Vector:** The attack exploited vulnerabilities in resources originating from both first-party and third-party sources, including browser extensions.

**Risk Assessment:**

* **Severity:** High – This attack demonstrates a significant potential for data breaches, reputational damage, and operational disruption.
* **Likelihood:** Medium – The widespread use of polyfills and the demonstrated effectiveness of this attack vector suggest a continued risk.
* **Impact:** High – Potential for financial loss, legal liabilities, and loss of customer trust.

**Recommendations:**

1. **Immediate Review of Third-Party Dependencies:** Conduct a thorough audit of all third-party JavaScript libraries and polyfills used across our customer applications.
2. **Strengthen Supply Chain Security:** Implement stricter controls over third-party software, including vulnerability scanning, code review, and vendor risk management.
3. **Enhance Monitoring & Detection:** Leverage solutions like Akamai’s Client-Side Protection & Compliance to continuously monitor JavaScript executions for suspicious behavior.
4. **Proactive Communication:** Maintain open communication with customers regarding this threat and provide guidance on strengthening their own security posture.

**Moving Forward:**

We are actively collaborating with our customers to fully remediate the impact of this attack and prevent future occurrences.  We will continue to monitor the threat landscape and provide ongoing updates and guidance.
