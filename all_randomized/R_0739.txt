---

**1. Executive Summary**

The ShinyHunters threat actor is exploiting trusted employee access to Salesforce applications to conduct data extortion attacks, posing a significant risk to our organization’s customer data and operational capabilities. This tactic leverages social engineering and insider threats to gain unauthorized access, potentially leading to substantial financial losses and reputational damage. We must prioritize immediate action to prevent further compromise and potential disruption of critical business processes.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

A successful ShinyHunters attack could result in substantial financial losses due to ransom demands, costs associated with data recovery and remediation, and potential legal liabilities related to data breaches. Furthermore, the compromise of customer data would severely damage our organization’s reputation and erode customer trust, leading to a decline in sales and market share. Regulatory scrutiny and potential fines related to data protection regulations would also represent a significant financial and operational burden.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The ShinyHunters threat actor is targeting high-privilege user accounts within cloud-based project management and engineering collaboration platforms, specifically focusing on Salesforce applications. This exposes our organization’s customer relationship management (CRM) system and related data, impacting sales, marketing, and customer service operations. The potential for widespread data exfiltration and disruption of critical business processes represents a broad operational risk across multiple departments.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Implement Enhanced Monitoring:** Immediately deploy enhanced monitoring across all Salesforce applications to detect suspicious user activity, including unusual data access patterns and unauthorized application connections. This will provide early warning signs of potential compromise and enable rapid response.
*   **Strengthen Access Controls:** Enforce strict access controls, limiting employee access to sensitive data on a need-to-know basis and implementing multi-factor authentication (MFA) across all Salesforce applications. This will reduce the attack surface and limit the potential damage from a compromised account.
*   **Conduct Employee Training:** Conduct comprehensive employee training programs focused on recognizing and avoiding social engineering attacks, particularly voice-based phishing attempts. Educating employees on how to verify unexpected IT support calls is crucial to preventing credential compromise.
*   **Enforce Secure Connection Protocols:** Mandate the use of secure connection protocols (e.g., HTTPS) for all Salesforce application access and implement policies to restrict access from untrusted networks. This will help prevent attackers from intercepting sensitive data during transmission.
*   **Implement Data Loss Prevention (DLP) Measures:** Deploy DLP measures to monitor and prevent the exfiltration of sensitive customer data from Salesforce applications. This will provide an additional layer of protection against data theft and potential regulatory violations.
