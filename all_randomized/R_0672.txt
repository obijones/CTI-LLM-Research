**BOTTOM LINE: Immediately implement enhanced monitoring of external communications to detect and respond to potential CL0P ransomware activity targeting our organization.**

---

**1. Executive Summary**

CL0P ransomware actors have recently stolen sensitive information from a GoAnywhere MFT resource, and are actively attempting to negotiate with victims. This represents a significant risk to our organization, potentially leading to data breaches, financial losses, and reputational damage.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

A successful CL0P attack could result in substantial operational disruption, including the inability to process transactions or access critical data. The threat of public disclosure of stolen information could severely damage our reputation and erode customer trust, leading to significant financial losses and potential regulatory fines related to data protection failures.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The threat actor is targeting systems that handle sensitive data, specifically GoAnywhere MFT resources. This exposes our organization to the risk of ransomware deployment across our operational systems, potentially disrupting key business processes such as customer service, financial operations, and supply chain management.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Immediately implement enhanced monitoring of external communications:** This will allow us to detect and respond to potential CL0P ransomware activity targeting our organization, including communication attempts from the identified contact email address.
*   **Review and strengthen remote access controls:** By auditing remote access tools and requiring authorized solutions only to be used from within our network over approved remote access solutions, we can reduce the attack surface and limit the potential for lateral movement.
*   **Implement application controls to manage and control execution of software:** This will help prevent unauthorized software from running and potentially spreading ransomware.
*   **Segment networks to prevent the spread of ransomware:** Network segmentation can help prevent the spread of ransomware by controlling traffic flows between—and access to—various subnetworks and by restricting adversary lateral movement.
*   **Promptly report any suspected incidents to the appropriate authorities:** Following the FBI and CISA guidance, reporting any suspected ransomware incidents will allow for coordinated investigation and mitigation efforts, and ensure we comply with reporting requirements.
