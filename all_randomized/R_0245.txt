**Executive Threat Intelligence Report – Ransomware Risk Assessment**

**1. Executive Summary**

LockBit 3.0 ransomware affiliates are actively targeting organizations across multiple critical infrastructure sectors, including financial services, and pose a significant risk to our operations. This threat actor’s observed tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) indicate a deliberate effort to gain access to sensitive data and disrupt business processes, potentially leading to substantial financial losses and reputational damage. We must immediately implement recommended mitigation strategies to reduce our exposure.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

A successful LockBit 3.0 attack could result in significant operational disruption, potentially halting critical financial transactions and impacting our ability to serve customers. The compromise of sensitive data, including customer information and financial records, could trigger substantial regulatory fines and penalties, as well as severe reputational damage impacting customer trust and market value. The potential for extortion demands further exacerbate the financial risk.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

This threat actor’s activity targets organizations utilizing Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software, impacting systems used to manage network traffic and access. Specifically, the threat actor’s observed TTPs indicate a focus on gaining administrative access to these systems, which are critical for maintaining network connectivity and securing sensitive data. This exposes our entire operational network to potential compromise.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Immediately isolate** all systems utilizing Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software to prevent further potential compromise. This will minimize the potential for the threat actor to gain broader access to our network and systems.
*   **Reimage** all compromised systems to ensure the removal of any malicious code or backdoors established by the threat actor. This action is critical to eliminate the threat actor’s persistence mechanisms.
*   **Create new account credentials** for all administrative accounts associated with the affected systems. This reduces the risk of the threat actor utilizing existing credentials to maintain access.
*   **Report the compromise** to the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) and CISA via the agency’s Incident Reporting System or its 24/7 Operations Center at report@cisa.gov or (888) 282-0870. This ensures that the threat actor’s activity is documented and tracked, and allows for collaboration with law enforcement and cybersecurity experts.
*   **Continue to monitor** systems for signs of compromise, utilizing techniques to hunt for malicious activity (see Additional Resources section). This proactive approach will help to identify and address any remaining threats.
