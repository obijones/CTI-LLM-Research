**Executive Threat Intelligence Report – Supply Chain Attack**

**1. Executive Summary**

A significant cyberattack, originating from compromised third-party resources, has targeted over 100,000 websites globally. This attack introduces malicious code into web applications, posing a direct risk to our organization’s operational continuity and potentially leading to financial losses and reputational damage. We recommend immediate action to mitigate the risk of similar attacks impacting our operations.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

This supply chain attack presents a serious threat to our business operations. If successful, malicious code could disrupt critical online services, leading to significant downtime and lost revenue. Furthermore, a compromised website could damage our brand reputation, eroding customer trust and potentially resulting in substantial financial losses. Finally, this type of attack could trigger regulatory scrutiny and associated fines related to data security and customer protection.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The attack targets websites that rely on third-party resources, including polyfills – pieces of code that enable modern website functionality on older browsers. This means that any website utilizing these compromised resources is vulnerable. Specifically, the attack impacts the delivery of online services and the functionality of websites that depend on these third-party components. This exposes a broad range of customer-facing operations and potentially impacts our ability to securely deliver services to our clients.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Immediately review all third-party resource integrations:** Conduct a thorough audit of all third-party resources used across our digital platforms to identify any potential vulnerabilities. This will allow us to quickly assess the scope of our exposure.
*   **Implement enhanced monitoring of third-party resources:** Deploy real-time monitoring solutions to detect and block the execution of suspicious scripts originating from third-party sources. This proactive approach will minimize the potential for malicious code to be injected into our systems.
*   **Enforce strict access controls for third-party resources:** Limit the access rights granted to third-party resources to the absolute minimum required for their functionality. This will reduce the potential damage if a resource is compromised.
*   **Prioritize rapid response to detected threats:** Establish a clear incident response plan to quickly identify, contain, and remediate any compromised websites or applications. This rapid response capability is critical to minimizing the impact of an attack.
*   **Collaborate with Akamai Client-Side Protection & Compliance:** Engage with Akamai’s expertise to gain insights into the attack vector and implement proactive measures to protect our systems from similar threats. Their monitoring capabilities can provide early warning of potential attacks.
