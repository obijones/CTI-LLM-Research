**Executive Threat Intelligence Report: SonicWall Zero-Day Vulnerability**

**To:** Senior Business Leadership
**From:** [Your Name/Cybersecurity Analyst Team]
**Date:** October 26, 2026
**Subject:** Critical Threat – Active Exploitation of SonicWall VPN Vulnerability

**Executive Summary:**

We’ve identified a highly active and critical cyber threat targeting SonicWall seventh-generation firewall appliances. A zero-day vulnerability is being exploited, allowing attackers to bypass Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and deploy ransomware, most notably Akira. The situation demands immediate and decisive action to mitigate potential damage. This report outlines the key findings, recommended actions, and ongoing monitoring requirements.

**Key Findings:**

*   **Active Exploitation:** Multiple incidents linked to this vulnerability are currently underway, with at least 28 confirmed cases as of August 6, 2026.  This represents a significant and escalating risk.
*   **Attack Vector:** The primary attack vector involves compromising SonicWall VPNs (default port 4433 – verify your device’s port configuration).  Attackers are leveraging known vulnerabilities in firmware versions 7.2.0-7015 and earlier.
*   **Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs):**  Once inside the network, attackers are employing a range of techniques including:
    *   **Privilege Escalation:** Immediately gaining administrative access via overprivileged LDAP or service accounts (e.g., “sonicwall,” “LDAPAdmin”).
    *   **Lateral Movement:** Utilizing techniques to move within the network after initial compromise.
    *   **Credential Theft:** Targeting user accounts with VPN access.
    *   **Malicious Driver Deployment:**  Employing BYOVD attacks using drivers (rwdrv.sys and hlpdrv.sys) to evade security tools.
    *   **Malicious Activity:**  Including deleting Volume Shadow Copies via WMI (powershell.exe -Command "Get-WmiObject firewalls") to disrupt forensic investigations.
*   **Indicators of Compromise (IOCs):** The following IP addresses have been associated with the threat activity: 45.86.208.240, 77.247.126.239, 104.238.205.105, 104.238.220.216, 181.215.182.64.  (Note: We strongly recommend monitoring for any additional IOCs as they emerge).

**Immediate Recommended Actions:**

1.  **Disable SonicWall VPN Access:** This is the *highest priority* action. Immediately disable SSL VPN access on all SonicWall appliances until a patch and official guidance are released.
2.  **Restrict Access:** If VPN access is business-critical, implement a strict allow-list of trusted IP addresses.
3.  **Network Segmentation:** Segment the network to prevent a compromised SonicWall appliance from providing immediate access to critical servers (e.g., domain controllers).
4.  **Account Audits:** Conduct thorough audits of service accounts, particularly those with VPN access, ensuring they adhere to the principle of least privilege.  Rotate credentials immediately.
5.  **Monitor for IOCs:**  Continuously monitor your environment for the listed IP addresses and the malicious drivers (rwdrv.sys and hlpdrv.sys).
6.  **Update Firmware:**  Monitor for and deploy the official SonicWall security advisory updates as soon as they are released.
