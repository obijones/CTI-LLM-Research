This threat affects any organization utilizing Cisco IOS XE devices, particularly those with internet-facing network infrastructure. The scope is broad, encompassing a wide range of network devices including routers, switches, and firewalls. Given the widespread adoption of Cisco IOS XE, the potential number of impacted organizations is substantial. It’s critical to recognize that even devices not directly connected to the internet can be compromised and used as stepping stones to attack other systems within our network.
