**Cyber Threat Intelligence Report: Polyfill.io Supply Chain Attack – Executive Summary**

1.  **Executive Summary**

We’ve identified a significant and rapidly evolving cyber threat originating from a compromised third-party service – polyfill.io. This incident highlights a critical vulnerability in how many organizations rely on external services to enhance their web applications. Essentially, malicious actors gained access to a widely used component and leveraged it to inject harmful code into numerous websites, targeting a broad range of customers. This represents a sophisticated supply chain attack, and our immediate priority is to understand the full extent of the compromise and mitigate further damage.

2.  **Potential Business Impact**

The potential impact of this attack is substantial and multi-faceted. Beyond the immediate risk of compromised user data, this incident threatens our customers’ brand reputation, customer trust, and operational stability. The widespread nature of the compromise – affecting over 40 customers – indicates a significant disruption to their online services. Furthermore, the attack demonstrates a vulnerability in our own ecosystem, potentially exposing our clients to similar risks from other third-party integrations. The cost of remediation, including incident response, customer notifications, and potential legal ramifications, could be considerable. We must act swiftly to minimize these risks.

3.  **Affected Systems / Scope**

The attack exploited a vulnerability within polyfill.io, a service used by organizations to provide compatibility for older browsers. This resulted in malicious code being injected into the web applications of numerous customers. The scope of the compromise extends across a wide range of industries, impacting both large enterprises and smaller businesses. Critically, the attack leveraged a supply chain vulnerability – meaning the risk wasn’t limited to the compromised service itself, but extended to all organizations that relied upon it. The attack targeted JavaScript resources, a common element in modern web applications, making it difficult to detect and remove.

We are taking immediate steps to contain the damage and prevent further exploitation. Our primary focus is on rapid customer notification and support.
