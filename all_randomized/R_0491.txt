**Key Findings & Analysis:**

* **Containment Actions:** Immediate containment actions were taken, including:
    * Revoking compromised session cookies.
    * Rolling back any MFA modifications made by the attacker.
    * Continuous monitoring for further compromised users and changes in phishing patterns.
* **Technical Mitigation Recommendations (Immediate):**
    * **Continuous Access Evaluation (CAE):** Implement or strengthen CAE to continuously assess user access based on multiple signals, reducing reliance solely on MFA.
    * **Advanced Anti-Phishing Solutions:** Ensure our existing anti-phishing solutions are fully up-to-date and actively scanning incoming emails and visited websites.  Specifically, leverage web browser security features to block malicious sites.
    * **Enhanced Monitoring:** Increase the scope of our security monitoring, focusing on suspicious login attempts with unusual characteristics (location, ISP, user agent, anonymizer services).
* **Underlying Vulnerability:** The attack highlights the potential for domain compromise and the need for robust domain security controls.

**Recommendations for Ongoing Action:**

* **Investment in Threat Hunting:**  We recommend increasing investment in proactive threat hunting capabilities, mirroring the approach used by Microsoft Defender Experts, to identify and neutralize emerging threats before they impact our organization.
* **Employee Awareness Training:** Reinforce employee awareness training regarding phishing attacks, emphasizing the importance of verifying requests for sensitive information and reporting suspicious activity.
* **Continuous Security Assessment:** Conduct a comprehensive security assessment to identify and address any vulnerabilities that could be exploited by attackers.

This incident underscores the evolving sophistication of cyber threats and the necessity of a proactive, layered security approach.
