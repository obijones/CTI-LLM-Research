**BOTTOM LINE: Immediately initiate a review of all third-party vendor relationships to mitigate the risk of data breaches and operational disruptions.**

**1. Executive Summary**

A significant ransomware attack targeting Change Healthcare has created a substantial risk to the broader healthcare ecosystem. The attack resulted in the theft of protected health information potentially impacting up to one in three Americans, and could severely disrupt critical healthcare operations. This necessitates immediate action to safeguard sensitive data and minimize potential business disruption.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

This incident poses a serious threat to our organization’s operations and financial stability. The potential for widespread data breaches involving sensitive patient information could trigger significant regulatory fines (related to HIPAA compliance), lead to substantial legal liabilities, and severely damage our reputation with customers and partners. Furthermore, the disruption to healthcare services – including medication delivery and patient care – could result in significant financial losses due to operational downtime and potential legal claims.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The ransomware attack has exposed vulnerabilities across Change Healthcare’s systems, impacting the processing of protected health information. This directly threatens the continuity of operations for healthcare providers reliant on Change Healthcare’s services, including those involved in patient care, billing, and data management. The scale of the potential impact is considerable, given Change Healthcare’s extensive reach within the U.S. healthcare sector.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Prioritize Vendor Risk Assessment:** Immediately initiate a comprehensive review of all third-party vendor relationships, focusing on those with access to sensitive data or critical systems. This review should assess the security posture of these vendors and identify potential weaknesses that could be exploited.
*   **Enhance Cybersecurity Spending:** Increase investment in cybersecurity measures, specifically targeting areas identified as weaknesses by recent audits and assessments. This includes bolstering defenses against ransomware attacks and improving incident response capabilities.
*   **Strengthen Data Breach Notification Protocols:** Establish and test robust data breach notification procedures to ensure timely and accurate communication with affected parties and regulatory agencies, as required by HIPAA.
*   **Review Board Oversight:** Conduct a thorough review of the governance and oversight of cybersecurity risk management, particularly focusing on the expertise and experience of the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and the board of directors.
*   **Monitor Regulatory Developments:** Closely monitor regulatory developments and guidance related to cybersecurity and data protection, particularly those issued by the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) and the Department of Justice (DOJ), to ensure compliance and preparedness.
