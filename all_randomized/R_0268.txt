**Executive Threat Intelligence Report – Ransomware Risk**

**1. Executive Summary**

LockBit 3.0 ransomware affiliates are actively targeting organizations across multiple critical infrastructure sectors, posing a significant and immediate risk to our operations. This threat actor’s observed activity indicates a potential for substantial disruption to our business processes, leading to potential financial losses and reputational damage. We must take immediate action to mitigate this risk and protect our organization’s assets.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

A successful LockBit 3.0 attack could result in significant operational disruption, potentially halting critical financial transactions and impacting our ability to serve our customers. The potential for data loss, including sensitive customer information, carries substantial financial consequences due to regulatory fines and remediation costs. Furthermore, a breach could severely damage our reputation, eroding customer trust and impacting our market value.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

This threat actor’s activity targets organizations utilizing Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software, impacting systems used to manage network traffic and access. This exposes our business to the risk of unauthorized access to our network and data, potentially disrupting core business functions reliant on these systems, including customer account management and financial reporting.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Immediately assess all Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software** – This proactive step is necessary to determine the extent of potential compromise and identify any systems that may be vulnerable to attack.
*   **Reimage compromised hosts** – Taking this action will remove any malicious code or unauthorized access points established by the threat actor, preventing further exploitation.
*   **Create new account credentials** – This will eliminate the risk of the threat actor utilizing existing privileged accounts to maintain access to our systems.
*   **Report the compromise to FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)** – Reporting the incident to the appropriate authorities will facilitate investigation and potential recovery efforts, while also demonstrating our commitment to security best practices.
*   **State, local, tribal, or territorial government (SLTT) entities can also report to MS-ISAC** – This ensures comprehensive reporting and coordination with relevant cybersecurity resources.
