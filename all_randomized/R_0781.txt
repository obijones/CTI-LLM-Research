**BOTTOM LINE: Immediately initiate a review of all third-party vendor relationships to mitigate the risk of data breaches and operational disruption.**

---

**1. Executive Summary**

A significant cyberattack targeting Change Healthcare, a major U.S. healthcare data processor, represents a critical threat to the stability of the nation’s healthcare system. The attack, involving the theft of 4TB of data and the potential exposure of personal information for up to 1 in 3 Americans, creates a substantial risk of operational disruption, financial loss, and reputational damage for organizations reliant on Change Healthcare’s services.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

This incident poses a severe risk to healthcare providers and patients. The disruption to Change Healthcare’s operations could lead to delays in patient care, medication shortages, and significant financial losses for healthcare organizations. Furthermore, the potential exposure of sensitive patient data—including military personnel information—creates a serious risk of identity theft, fraud, and national security implications, demanding immediate attention and potentially triggering substantial regulatory fines.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The attack has impacted Change Healthcare’s systems, which process data for a wide range of healthcare providers and organizations across the United States. This exposes critical business processes related to patient scheduling, billing, and data management. The scale of the breach—potentially affecting data for one-third of the U.S. population—represents a systemic risk to the entire healthcare ecosystem, highlighting the vulnerability of a single, dominant player.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Immediately Assess Vendor Risk:** Direct Change Healthcare to provide a comprehensive assessment of its third-party vendor relationships, focusing on identifying and mitigating potential weaknesses in their security posture.
*   **Enhance Data Breach Response Protocols:** Direct Change Healthcare to refine its incident response plan to ensure rapid notification of affected parties and effective containment measures, aligning with regulatory requirements.
*   **Prioritize MFA Implementation:** Direct Change Healthcare to accelerate the implementation of multi-factor authentication (MFA) across all external-facing systems, recognizing that this is a fundamental security control that could have prevented this attack.
*   **Conduct Comprehensive Security Audits:** Direct Change Healthcare to conduct thorough security audits of its internal systems and current vendors, ensuring alignment with industry best practices and regulatory standards.
*   **Establish Enhanced Oversight:** Direct CMS to increase oversight of third-party healthcare vendors, particularly those with significant market share, to proactively identify and address potential vulnerabilities and systemic risks.
