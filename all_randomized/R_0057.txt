**Executive Report: Urgent Action Required – CVE-2024-3400 Exploitation**

**To:** Senior Business Leadership
**From:** [Your Name/Cybersecurity Analyst Team]
**Date:** May 17, 2026
**Subject:** Critical Vulnerability Exploitation – PAN-OS GlobalProtect

**Executive Summary:**

We are reporting a critical, actively exploited zero-day vulnerability (CVE-2024-3400) impacting Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS devices, specifically versions 120.2, 11.0, and 11.1.  Kroll’s analysis indicates widespread exploitation by multiple actors, posing a significant and immediate threat to our network security.  The vulnerability allows for unauthenticated remote code execution with root privileges, representing a high-impact risk.  Immediate action is required to mitigate this threat.

**Key Findings:**

* **Vulnerability Details:** CVE-2024-3400 is a command injection vulnerability within the GlobalProtect feature of PAN-OS. The vulnerability stems from improper sanitization of SessionIDs, allowing attackers to execute arbitrary commands.
* **Active Exploitation:** Kroll has observed widespread exploitation of this vulnerability, with activity originating from a geographically diverse range of IP addresses.  This indicates a coordinated and ongoing attack campaign.
* **Affected PAN-OS Versions:** The vulnerability affects PAN-OS 120.2, PAN-OS 11.0 and PAN-OS 11.1.
* **Immediate Mitigation:**
    * **Patching is Critical:** We strongly recommend immediate patching of all affected PAN-OS devices to the latest versions (10.2.9-h1, PAN-OS 11.0.4-h1, PAN-OS 11.1.2-h3, and later). Hotfixes are available and should be deployed without delay.
    * **Threat Prevention Blocking:** Palo Alto Networks customers can utilize Threat IDs 95187, 95189 and 95191 to block attacks.
* **Ongoing Investigation:** Kroll is actively investigating the source of the attacks and analyzing the techniques used by the attackers. We are monitoring IOCs (Indicators of Compromise) and have added them to our detection database.
* **Potential Impact:** Successful exploitation could lead to complete network compromise, data exfiltration, and disruption of critical business operations.

**Recommendations:**

1. **Prioritize Patching:** Immediately initiate a coordinated effort to deploy the latest PAN-OS patches across all affected devices.
2. **Enhanced Monitoring:** Increase monitoring of network traffic and system logs for suspicious activity. Utilize Palo Alto’s CLI commands to identify potential indicators of compromise.
3. **Threat Intelligence Sharing:** Share this intelligence with relevant internal teams (IT Operations, Security Operations, Legal) and external partners.
4. **Continued Monitoring:** Kroll will continue to monitor the situation and provide updates as new information becomes available.

**Next Steps:**

We are coordinating with Palo Alto Networks and our incident response team to ensure rapid deployment of patches and ongoing threat detection. We will provide further updates as the situation evolves.
