Akamai’s Client-Side Protection & Compliance solution successfully identified and mitigated this attack in real-time, demonstrating the effectiveness of our proactive defenses.

* **Attack Mechanism:** Attackers utilized techniques like sampling (targeting specific device types and referrer data) to refine their attacks and evade detection. The malicious JavaScript communicated with a server at googie-anaiytics[.]com.
