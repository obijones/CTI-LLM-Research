**Executive Summary:**

Rapid7 has identified a significant and evolving threat landscape surrounding Fortinet Firewalls. A newly disclosed zero-day vulnerability (CVE-2024-55591) is being actively exploited in the wild, alongside a substantial data leak originating from 2022. This situation presents a critical risk to organizations utilizing Fortinet firewalls, demanding immediate attention and proactive mitigation.  The data leak, involving approximately 15,000 FortiGate firewalls, contains sensitive information including IP addresses, passwords, and configuration data.

**Key Findings:**

* **Active Zero-Day Exploitation:** CVE-2024-55591, an authentication bypass vulnerability, is being leveraged by threat actors. Arctic Wolf and Rapid7 MDR threat hunters have observed scanning and reconnaissance activity, but not yet full exploitation.
* **Significant Data Leak (2022 Data):** A threat actor, “Belsen Group,” published a large trove of FortiGate firewall data, primarily from 2022. This data represents a significant risk due to potential compromise of older systems.
* **Potential Root Cause - Older Vulnerability:** Analysis suggests that CVE-2022-40684 (a previously disclosed FortiOS zero-day) may have been the initial access vector for the data leak.
* **Ongoing Exploitation:** CVE-2024-55591 is currently being exploited, highlighting the urgency of remediation.
* **Immediate Mitigation Steps:**
    * **Urgent Patching:**  Rapid7 recommends immediate patching of FortiOS 7.0.0 through 7.0.16 and FortiProxy 7.2.0 through 7.2.12.
    * **Password Changes:**  Mandatory password resets for all administrative and local user accounts on Fortinet firewalls.
    * **MFA Implementation:**  Strongly recommend implementing Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all local user accounts.
    * **Network Monitoring:** Increased monitoring for suspicious activity, particularly related to administrative logins and configuration changes.

**Risk Assessment:**

* **Severity:** High – Due to the active exploitation of a zero-day vulnerability and the availability of sensitive data.
* **Impact:** Potential for significant data breaches, unauthorized access to network resources, and reputational damage.
* **Likelihood:** High – Given the known exploitation and the large volume of compromised data.
