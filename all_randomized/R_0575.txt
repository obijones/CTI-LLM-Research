**BOTTOM LINE: Immediately implement enhanced monitoring of all international financial transactions to mitigate the risk of significant financial loss and reputational damage resulting from business email compromise attacks.**

---

**1. Executive Summary**

A global business email compromise (BEC) scam targeting financial institutions is underway, facilitated by a mechanism allowing for the interception of payments. This activity poses a direct threat to the organization’s financial stability and operational continuity, potentially resulting in the loss of millions of dollars and significant reputational damage.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

This BEC campaign represents a serious risk to the organization’s financial operations. Successful exploitation could lead to substantial financial losses through fraudulent transfers, disrupt critical payment processing systems, and trigger regulatory scrutiny due to non-compliance with financial reporting requirements. Furthermore, a successful attack could severely damage the organization’s reputation, eroding trust with customers and partners.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The core business risk lies within the organization’s international payment processes. Specifically, the vulnerability exists in the potential for fraudulent emails impersonating legitimate suppliers to redirect funds. This compromises the integrity of the payment chain, impacting all transactions involving external suppliers and potentially exposing the entire global financial network. The disruption of this process would halt international payments, impacting trade and supply chain operations.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Implement Enhanced Transaction Monitoring:** Immediately deploy enhanced monitoring of all international financial transactions, focusing on payments to newly established suppliers or those with altered email addresses, to detect and prevent fraudulent transfers.
*   **Strengthen Supplier Verification Protocols:** Implement stricter verification procedures for all new suppliers, including multi-factor authentication and independent confirmation of supplier details, to reduce the likelihood of impersonation.
*   **Reinforce Employee Training:** Conduct immediate and comprehensive training for all employees involved in financial transactions, emphasizing the risks of BEC scams and the importance of verifying all requests for payment, particularly those received via email.
*   **Activate the INTERPOL I-GRIP Mechanism:** Immediately engage INTERPOL’s I-GRIP mechanism to facilitate rapid investigation and potential recovery of funds in the event of a confirmed attack, leveraging the organization’s global network to swiftly identify and halt fraudulent payments.
*   **Conduct a Rapid Risk Assessment:** Initiate a rapid assessment of the organization’s overall vulnerability to BEC attacks, identifying potential weaknesses in existing security controls and developing a prioritized action plan for remediation.
