**Executive Threat Intelligence Report: SonicWall Zero-Day Vulnerability**

**Date:** October 26, 2023 (Based on provided context – assuming current date for reporting)
**Prepared by:** Cybersecurity Analyst

**Subject:** Urgent: Active Exploitation of SonicWall VPN Vulnerability – High Risk

**Executive Summary:**

We are facing a highly active and evolving cyber threat centered around a zero-day vulnerability in SonicWall seventh-generation firewall appliances, specifically those running SSL VPN. Threat actors are actively exploiting this vulnerability to gain unauthorized access to networks, deploying ransomware (likely Akira), and potentially disrupting critical operations. Immediate and decisive action is required to mitigate this risk.

**Key Findings & Threat Landscape:**

* **Confirmed Vulnerability:** A zero-day vulnerability exists within SonicWall VPN firmware versions 7.2.0-7015 and earlier. This vulnerability allows attackers to bypass MFA and gain control of the device.
* **Active Exploitation:** Multiple incidents have been confirmed, including one involving a significant intrusion on July 25th where an attacker leveraged the vulnerability to delete Volume Shadow Copies via WMI, a common tactic for data destruction.
* **Attack Techniques:** The observed attack chain involves:
    * **Initial Breach:** Exploitation of the SonicWall appliance itself.
    * **Lateral Movement:** Abuse of privileged accounts (LDAPAdmin, sonicwall) to gain administrative access.
    * **Detection Evasion:** Use of BYOVD attacks, specifically the `rwdrv.sys` and `hlpdrv.sys` drivers, to bypass security tools.
    * **Post-Exploitation:**  Standard ransomware deployment (likely Akira), credential theft, and potentially further reconnaissance and data exfiltration.
* **Indicator of Compromise (IOCs):** The following IP addresses have been associated with the threat activity: 45.86.208.240, 77.247.126.239, 104.238.205.105, 104.238.220.216, 181.215.182.64.  (Note:  We strongly recommend monitoring these IPs and adding them to your blocklists.)
* **Current Status:** GuidePoint Security and Huntress have identified at least 28 incidents linked to this activity as of August 6, 2025. SonicWall has released a security advisory and updated mitigation steps.

**Immediate Actions Required:**

1. **Disable SonicWall VPN:** This is the *highest priority* action. Immediately disable SSL VPN access on all SonicWall appliances.
2. **Network Segmentation:** Segment the network to prevent a compromised SonicWall from immediately accessing critical servers.
3. **Account Auditing:**  Immediately review and rotate credentials for all service accounts, particularly those with access to the SonicWall or Active Directory. Enforce the principle of least privilege.
4. **Threat Hunting:** Utilize the provided IOCs to actively scan your environment for signs of compromise.
5. **Firmware Update:**  Upgrade to the latest SonicWall firmware version as soon as patches are available.

**Risk Level:** Critical – This threat is actively being exploited and poses a significant risk to our organization’s data, systems, and operations.

* Continue to monitor this threat landscape closely.
