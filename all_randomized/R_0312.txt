This report details a significant cybersecurity incident involving the 3CX PBX software provider, highlighting a sophisticated supply chain attack orchestrated by a North Korean (likely Lazarus Group) actor. This incident underscores the critical vulnerability of software supply chains and the potential for nation-state actors to inflict substantial damage through seemingly legitimate software updates.

**Key Findings:**

* **Attack Vector:** The attack originated with a compromise of the Trading Technologies website, used by 3CX customers to download software updates. This represents a critical vulnerability in the software supply chain.
* **Malware:** The attacker deployed POOLRAT, a macOS backdoor capable of data collection, command execution, and file manipulation. This malware was previously identified and triaged by CISA and Mandiant.
* **Targeted Platform:** While initially focused on Windows, the attack successfully exploited macOS systems, demonstrating the attacker’s adaptability and expanding the potential impact.
* **Timeline of Events:**

    * **March 29, 2023:** CrowdStrike confirmed the supply chain attack, identifying malicious activity originating from a legitimate 3CXDesktopApp binary.
    * **April 1, 2023:** Objective-See Foundation detailed the macOS payload and analysis.
    * **Ongoing:** SentinelOne and Mandiant continued to provide updates and analysis, ultimately attributing the attack to the UNC4736 actor, linked to the Lazarus Group.
* **Impact:** The attack resulted in the infection of 3CX customers’ systems, potentially exposing sensitive data and disrupting business operations.
* **Root Cause:** The attack highlights a critical vulnerability in the software supply chain, where a compromised Trading Technologies website served as the initial entry point.

**Strategic Implications & Recommendations:**

* **Supply Chain Risk Management:** This incident necessitates a comprehensive review and strengthening of our supply chain risk management processes. This includes enhanced vendor due diligence, secure software development practices, and robust monitoring of software updates.
