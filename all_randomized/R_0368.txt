**1. Executive Summary**

Threat actors are exploiting collaboration platforms, specifically Microsoft Teams, to impersonate IT helpdesk staff and gain access to user accounts. This activity poses a significant risk of business disruption, financial fraud, and potential reputational damage to the organization.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

A successful attack could lead to unauthorized access to sensitive financial data, resulting in direct financial loss through fraudulent transactions. Furthermore, the disruption of critical business operations – such as payroll, vendor payments, and customer communications – would translate into substantial operational costs and potentially damage the organization’s reputation. The potential for regulatory scrutiny related to data breaches and compliance failures adds another layer of risk.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

This threat targets the organization’s ability to securely access and manage its systems and data. Specifically, the vulnerability lies in the potential for attackers to impersonate legitimate IT support personnel, leveraging trust relationships to gain access to user accounts. This compromises the security of all business processes that rely on secure access to information and systems, including financial transactions, customer data management, and internal communications.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Immediately Revoke Session Cookies:** Direct IT security personnel to immediately revoke all active session cookies associated with user accounts, effectively terminating any unauthorized access gained through the AiTM attack. This is a critical step to prevent further compromise.
*   **Roll Back Recent MFA Modifications:** Direct IT security personnel to roll back any recent changes made to Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) configurations, as the attacker exploited these changes to gain access.
*   **Initiate Account Freeze:** Direct IT security personnel to temporarily freeze all user accounts that have been identified as potentially compromised, preventing further access until a thorough investigation can be completed.
*   **Conduct Forensic Analysis:** Direct the security team to conduct a comprehensive forensic analysis of affected systems and user accounts to fully understand the scope of the attack and identify any additional vulnerabilities.
*   **Enhance User Awareness Training:** Direct the organization to reinforce user awareness training, specifically focusing on recognizing and reporting suspicious emails and requests for remote access, to prevent future attacks.
