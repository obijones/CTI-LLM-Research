**Executive Threat Intelligence Report – Cisco IOS XE WebUI Privesc Vulnerabilities**

**To:** Senior Business Leadership
**From:** [Your Name/Cybersecurity Analyst]
**Date:** October 26, 2023
**Subject:** Urgent Action Required: Cisco IOS XE WebUI Privesc Vulnerabilities – Active Exploitation

**Executive Summary:**

This report details a critical and ongoing threat targeting Cisco IOS XE devices due to previously disclosed vulnerabilities (CVE-2023-20198 and CVE-2023-20273).  We’ve observed active exploitation of these vulnerabilities by a sophisticated threat actor, supported by the release of proof-of-concept (POC) code and a subsequent surge in attack attempts.  Immediate action is required to mitigate the risk of unauthorized access and potential system compromise.

**Key Findings:**

* **Vulnerability:** The core issue revolves around a privilege escalation vulnerability within the Cisco IOS XE WebUI (Web Management User Interface).
* **Active Exploitation:**  Since the publication of POCs on October 30 & 31, 2023, we’ve seen a dramatic increase in exploitation attempts. This indicates a well-funded and coordinated attack campaign.
* **Threat Actor Tactics:** The threat actor is employing established tactics, including:
    * **Information Gathering:**  Initial reconnaissance involved commands mirroring those documented in the Appendix, including log clearing and user removal.
    * **Evidence Destruction:**  The actor attempted to cover their tracks by deleting logs and removing user accounts (cisco_tac_admin, cisco_sys_manager).
    * **Persistent Access:**  The actor established persistent access via deployment of a “BadCandy” web shell, indicating a move beyond simple exploitation to establishing a foothold.
* **Timeline of Activity:**  Suspicious activity began as early as September 18th, 2023, with the creation of the “cisco_tac_admin” account from a suspicious IP address.  Further activity was observed through October 1st, followed by a cleanup operation.
* **Cisco Response:** Cisco PSIRT has issued a security advisory and recommended immediate implementation of the outlined mitigations.  Talos Intelligence is actively tracking the exploitation and providing updates.
* **Severity:** High – This vulnerability allows for complete system compromise, posing a significant risk to our network infrastructure and data security.

**Recommendations – Immediate Action Required:**

1. **Disable HTTP/S Server:**  Immediately disable the HTTP/S server feature on all internet-facing Cisco IOS XE devices, as recommended by Cisco and aligned with U.S. government guidance.
2. **Patching:**  Apply the security patches released by Cisco as quickly as possible.
3. **Network Monitoring:**  Increase monitoring of network traffic for suspicious activity, specifically targeting the IP addresses associated with the threat actor.
4. **Vulnerability Scanning:** Conduct thorough vulnerability scans of all affected devices.
5. **Review Access Controls:**  Re-evaluate and strengthen access controls to all network devices.

**Conclusion:**

This situation represents a serious and evolving threat.  We must act decisively and swiftly to protect our systems.  We will continue to monitor the threat landscape and provide updates as new information becomes available.
