**Cyber Threat Intelligence Report – Business Email Compromise (BEC) Activity**

**2. Potential Business Impact**

The primary risk posed by this BEC activity is substantial financial loss. The sophistication of these scams means that employees, often trusting relationships, are being manipulated into transferring significant sums of money. Beyond direct financial loss, this activity carries reputational damage if detected, potentially eroding trust with our partners and clients. Furthermore, the time and resources required to investigate and remediate a successful BEC attack – including forensic analysis, legal counsel, and communication with stakeholders – will represent a considerable operational burden. The potential for disruption to business operations, stemming from employee distraction and investigation efforts, also needs to be considered.

Currently, the threat appears to be focused on targeting companies engaging in international transactions and those reliant on supplier relationships. The scope of this threat extends beyond a single company; it represents a systemic vulnerability across industries reliant on external communications and financial transactions. We must assume that our own systems and processes are equally vulnerable.

**4. Recommended Actions**

Immediate action is required to bolster our defenses. We recommend the following prioritized steps:
