**Executive Threat Intelligence Report: SonicWall Zero-Day Vulnerability**

**Date:** October 26, 2026 (Based on document publication dates)
**Prepared By:** Cybersecurity Analyst

**Subject:** Critical Threat – Active Exploitation of SonicWall VPN Vulnerability

**Executive Summary:**

We’ve identified a highly active and critical threat targeting SonicWall seventh-generation firewall appliances. A zero-day vulnerability is being exploited by sophisticated actors, primarily linked to Akira ransomware activity. This exploitation involves bypassing Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and deploying malicious activity. Immediate action is required to mitigate the risk of further compromise and potential data loss.

**Key Findings & Risks:**

*   **Active Exploitation:** Multiple incidents (at least 28 total) are confirmed, indicating a sustained and ongoing attack campaign.
*   **Vulnerability:** The vulnerability resides within SonicWall VPN firmware versions 7.2.0-7015 and earlier. This is a significant concern given the prevalence of these devices.
*   **Attack Vector:** The initial breach occurs through the SonicWall appliance itself, followed by a rapid sequence of post-exploitation activities.
*   **Tactics Observed:** Threat actors are employing a range of techniques, including:
    *   **Privilege Escalation:** Abusing overprivileged LDAP and service accounts (e.g., “sonicwall,” “LDAPAdmin”) to gain administrative access.
    *   **Lateral Movement:** Utilizing techniques to move within the network after initial compromise.
    *   **Credential Theft:** Targeting user accounts, particularly those with access to the VPN.
    *   **Malware Deployment:**  Evidence suggests the deployment of ransomware (likely Akira) following initial access.
    *   **Driver Exploitation:** Utilizing "Bring Your Own Vulnerable Driver" (BYOVD) attacks, specifically the `rwdrv.sys` and `hlpdrv.sys` drivers, to evade security controls.
*   **Immediate Actions Recommended:**
    *   **Disable SonicWall VPN Access:** This is the highest priority.
    *   **Network Segmentation:** Isolate SonicWall appliances to prevent lateral movement.
    *   **Account Audits:** Immediately review and rotate credentials for all service accounts, particularly those with VPN access.
    *   **Firmware Update:**  Upgrade to the latest SonicWall firmware version (as soon as guidance is provided).
    *   **Monitor for IOCs:**  Actively monitor your environment for Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) – specifically, the IP addresses listed in the document (45.86.208.240, 77.247.126.239, 104.238.205.105, 104.238.220.216, 181.215.182.64) and the malicious drivers (`rwdrv.sys`, `hlpdrv.sys`).

**Recommendations:**

*   **Activate Incident Response Plan:** Immediately implement your organization’s incident response plan.
*   **External Reconnaissance:** Utilize the provided external reconnaissance tool ([https://.huntress.io/account/external_recon](https://.huntress.io/account/external_recon)) to identify SonicWall devices within your network.
*   **Continuous Monitoring:** Maintain heightened vigilance and continue to monitor for any suspicious activity.
