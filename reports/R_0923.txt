**BOTTOM LINE: Immediately initiate a review of all third-party vendor relationships to mitigate the risk of data breaches and operational disruption.**

**1. Executive Summary**

A significant cyberattack targeting Change Healthcare has created a substantial risk to the broader healthcare ecosystem and potentially to financial institutions that rely on its systems. The attack, perpetrated by a ransomware group (ALPHV/Blackcat), resulted in the theft of protected health information impacting up to one in three Americans, creating a serious threat to patient safety and operational continuity. This necessitates immediate action to safeguard sensitive data and prevent further disruption.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

This incident poses a severe threat to business operations, potentially leading to significant financial losses due to operational downtime, remediation costs, and potential regulatory fines. The exposure of protected health information creates a substantial reputational risk, eroding trust with patients and partners. Furthermore, the potential for identity theft and fraud stemming from compromised data represents a direct financial liability.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The attack has impacted Change Healthcare’s systems, which are used by numerous healthcare providers and potentially financial institutions. This disruption affects the ability of providers to access patient records, process claims, and deliver care, directly impacting patient outcomes and operational efficiency. The scale of the breach – potentially affecting one in three Americans – amplifies the risk across a vast network of healthcare organizations.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Prioritize Vendor Risk Management:** Immediately conduct a comprehensive review of all third-party suppliers and financial intermediaries utilized by Change Healthcare to identify and address potential vulnerabilities. (This action directly mitigates the risk of data breaches originating from external sources.)
*   **Enhance Cybersecurity Spending:** Increase investment in cybersecurity measures, specifically focusing on bolstering defenses against ransomware attacks and data breaches. (This action directly addresses the increased effort and spending highlighted in the report.)
*   **Implement Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Ensure the widespread implementation of MFA across all external-facing systems, as recommended by Sen. Wyden, to prevent unauthorized access. (This action directly addresses the identified failure to implement MFA.)
*   **Conduct System Audits:** Mandate immediate audits of internal systems and current vendor relationships to identify and remediate security gaps. (This action directly addresses the 44% of respondents who conducted internal system audits.)
*   **Establish Breach Notification Protocols:** Develop and implement robust breach notification protocols, aligning with regulatory requirements and ensuring timely communication with affected parties, as required by HIPAA. (This action directly addresses the need for rapid recovery and communication outlined in the report.)
