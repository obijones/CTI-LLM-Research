**Subject:** Critical Vulnerability Disclosure – Juniper SRX Firewalls

**Executive Summary:**

We’ve identified a significant and rapidly evolving cybersecurity risk impacting our network infrastructure due to vulnerabilities within Juniper SRX firewalls. Specifically, CVE-2023-36844 and CVE-2023-36845, discovered and actively exploited by watchTowr Labs, allow for Remote Code Execution (RCE) with a combined CVSS score of 9.8. This represents a critical threat with the potential for substantial disruption and data compromise.  The ease of exploitation, coupled with the prevalence of Juniper SRX devices, warrants immediate attention and proactive mitigation.

**Key Findings:**

* **Vulnerability Details:** The vulnerabilities stem from a “Missing Authentication for Critical Function” (CVE-2023-36846) and a “PHP External Variable Modification” (CVE-2023-36845) flaw within the Junos OS running on Juniper SRX devices.
* **Exploitation Path:** watchTowr Labs demonstrated a method to combine these vulnerabilities to achieve RCE.  The process involves leveraging the J-Web interface, which is primarily written in PHP and exhibits code quality issues.
* **Severity:** The vulnerabilities are classified as “world ending” by watchTowr Labs, indicating a high potential impact.
* **Mitigation Recommendation (Immediate Action):**  Given the ease of exploitation, our immediate recommendation is to **disable the J-Web interface on all affected Juniper SRX devices.** This is the most effective and readily implementable short-term solution.
* **Longer-Term Actions:**
    * **Patching:**  We are monitoring Juniper’s security updates and will implement patched versions as soon as they are available.
    * **Continuous Monitoring:**  We will continue to monitor for further exploitation attempts and emerging threat intelligence related to this vulnerability.
    * **Attack Surface Management:**  This incident highlights the importance of robust attack surface management practices, including regular vulnerability scanning and penetration testing.

**Risk Assessment:**

* **Likelihood:** High – The vulnerabilities are publicly disclosed and demonstrated to be easily exploitable.
* **Impact:** Critical – RCE allows an attacker to gain complete control of the device, potentially leading to data exfiltration, system compromise, and network disruption.

**Next Steps:**

* **Immediate Implementation:**  Task IT Operations with disabling the J-Web interface on all SRX devices.
* **Patch Deployment:**  Coordinate with the IT team to schedule and execute the patch deployment as soon as it’s available.
