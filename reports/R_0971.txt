**BOTTOM LINE: Immediately implement enhanced monitoring and incident response procedures to mitigate the risk of ransomware attacks targeting critical business operations.**

---

**1. Executive Summary**

LockBit 3.0 ransomware affiliates are actively targeting organizations across multiple critical infrastructure sectors, including financial services, posing a significant and immediate threat to our operations. This activity represents a direct risk to our ability to conduct business, potentially leading to operational disruption and financial loss. We must take proactive steps to protect our systems and data from this evolving threat.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

A successful LockBit 3.0 attack could result in substantial operational disruption, halting critical financial transactions and impacting our ability to serve our customers. Furthermore, the compromise of sensitive data could trigger significant financial losses due to remediation costs, regulatory fines, and potential legal liabilities. The reputational damage associated with a ransomware attack would erode customer trust and negatively impact our market position.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

This threat actor is targeting organizations utilizing Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software, which are integral to our operational infrastructure. This exposes our business processes that rely on these systems, including data processing, customer access, and internal communications. The potential for widespread disruption across multiple business functions is a critical concern.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Prioritize Immediate System Assessment:** Immediately conduct a thorough assessment of all systems utilizing Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software to identify potential vulnerabilities and assess the extent of the risk.
*   **Implement Enhanced Monitoring:** Implement enhanced monitoring procedures to detect any unusual activity or unauthorized access attempts targeting our systems. This will allow for rapid identification and containment of potential attacks.
*   **Strengthen Incident Response Procedures:** Immediately review and update our incident response plan to specifically address ransomware attacks, ensuring we have the necessary protocols and resources in place to effectively respond to a compromise.
*   **Verify Administrator Accounts:** Verify the security of all administrator accounts, including implementing multi-factor authentication and regularly reviewing access privileges to limit potential damage from compromised credentials.
*   **Report the Incident:** Report the suspected compromise to the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) at IC3.gov or CISA via the agency’s Incident Reporting System or its 24/7 Operations Center at report@cisa.gov or (888) 282-0870. State, local, tribal, or territorial government (SLTT) entities can also report to MS-ISAC (866-787-4722 or SOC@cisecurity.org).
