This vulnerability impacts any SonicWall seventh-generation firewall appliance that has SSL VPN enabled, particularly those running firmware versions 7.2.0-7015 and earlier. Given the widespread use of SonicWall firewalls across various industries, the potential scope of this vulnerability is substantial. We are currently seeing evidence of this attack targeting organizations utilizing VPN access, highlighting the critical importance of securing this functionality. It’s crucial to understand that this isn’t limited to a single department or location; the attack vector is network-wide.
