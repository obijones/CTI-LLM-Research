    * **Data Breach:** Theft of sensitive company data, including customer information, intellectual property, and financial records.
    * **Ransomware Attack:** Encryption of critical systems, leading to significant operational disruption and potential financial losses.
    * **Reputational Damage:** Negative publicity and loss of customer trust.

**Recommendations – Immediate Action Required:**
