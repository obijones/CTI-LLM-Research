**1. Executive Summary**

LockBit 3.0 ransomware affiliates are actively targeting organizations across multiple critical infrastructure sectors, including financial services, and pose a significant risk to our operations. This threat actor’s observed tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) indicate a deliberate focus on gaining administrative access to systems, potentially disrupting critical business processes and leading to data compromise. We must take immediate action to protect our business from this evolving threat.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

A successful LockBit 3.0 attack could result in substantial operational disruption, potentially halting critical financial transactions, delaying reporting, and impacting our ability to serve customers. Furthermore, the compromise of sensitive data could trigger significant financial losses due to remediation costs, regulatory fines, and reputational damage. The potential for extortion demands further amplifies the financial risk.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

This threat actor’s activity is focused on organizations utilizing Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software, which are integral to our network infrastructure and support numerous business functions, including data transmission and application delivery. Disruptions to these systems would directly impact our ability to process transactions, manage customer accounts, and maintain operational efficiency.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Prioritize Immediate System Assessment:** Immediately assess all systems utilizing Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software for evidence of compromise, assuming full administrative access has been gained.
*   **Implement Host Reimaging:** If a potential compromise is suspected or detected, immediately initiate the reimaging of any affected hosts to eliminate potential malware and restore systems to a secure state.
*   **Establish Enhanced Monitoring:** Implement enhanced monitoring protocols to detect unusual activity, such as unauthorized access attempts, unusual network connections, and suspicious processes running on systems.
*   **Strengthen Account Security:** Create new account credentials for all critical systems and implement multi-factor authentication to prevent unauthorized access.
*   **Report Incident Immediately:** Report any suspected compromise to the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) or CISA’s 24/7 Operations Center to facilitate investigation and potential assistance.
