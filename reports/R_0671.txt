* **Impact:** The attack resulted in the compromise of 3CX’s network, impacting a significant number of users and businesses reliant on the 3CX platform.

* **Supply Chain Risk:** This incident highlights the inherent risk associated with relying on third-party software. We must rigorously assess the security practices of our vendors.
