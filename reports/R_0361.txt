The leaked data, primarily from 2022, includes sensitive information such as IP addresses, passwords, and firewall configurations. While the immediate exploitation of CVE-2024-55591 appears focused on reconnaissance and scanning, the potential for further compromise remains extremely high.

* **Potential Attack Vector (CVE-2022-40684):** Initial analysis suggests that CVE-2022-40684 may have been the initial access vector used in the data leak, highlighting the importance of patching legacy vulnerabilities.
* **Threat Actor Activity:** The "Belsen Group" is responsible for the data leak, demonstrating a persistent and organized threat actor.
* **Immediate Mitigation Needed:**  Rapid7 recommends immediate action to mitigate the risk, including:

    * **Password Reset:** Force a complete password reset for all administrative and local user accounts on affected firewalls.
    * **MFA Implementation:** Immediately implement Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all firewall management interfaces.
