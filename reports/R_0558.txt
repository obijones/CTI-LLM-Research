**Executive Threat Intelligence Report: Polyfill.io Supply Chain Attack**

**To:** Senior Business Leadership
**From:** [Your Name/Cybersecurity Analysis Team]
**Date:** October 26, 2024
**Subject:** Urgent: Mitigating Risks Associated with Third-Party JavaScript Dependencies – Polyfill.io Incident

**Executive Summary:**

We’ve identified a significant and ongoing cyber threat stemming from a supply chain attack utilizing the polyfill.io service. This attack, impacting over 100,000 websites and potentially exposing our customers to significant risk, highlights critical vulnerabilities within modern web application development practices – specifically, the reliance on third-party JavaScript libraries and the potential for malicious code injection through these channels.  Akamai’s Client-Side Protection & Compliance solution successfully detected and mitigated the attack in real-time, demonstrating the effectiveness of our proactive defenses.

**Key Findings:**

* **The Attack:** The attack leveraged polyfill.io, a service providing browser compatibility code, to inject malicious JavaScript into websites. This allowed attackers to track user behavior, steal data, and potentially compromise user sessions.
* **Scale of Impact:** The incident affected over 100,000 websites, with some customers experiencing tens of thousands of impacted pages daily.
* **Attack Vector:** The attackers targeted resources originating from both first-party and third-party sources, including browser extensions.
* **Akamai’s Response:** Akamai’s Client-Side Protection & Compliance solution, operating *in-browser*, detected the malicious JavaScript execution and immediately prevented its impact by stopping the attack. This in-browser detection was crucial in minimizing the blast radius.
* **Vulnerability:** The core vulnerability lies in the widespread use of third-party JavaScript libraries and the lack of robust monitoring and control over their execution. The polyfill.io service itself was compromised, acting as a conduit for the attack.
* **Real-time Mitigation:** The solution’s ability to detect and block the malicious script within seconds is a key differentiator and demonstrates the value of proactive threat detection.

**Strategic Implications & Recommendations:**

* **Increased Vigilance:** We must maintain heightened awareness of supply chain risks and the potential for compromised third-party components.
* **Third-Party Risk Management:** Implement a more rigorous process for evaluating and monitoring third-party JavaScript dependencies. This includes:
    * **Vendor Security Assessments:** Conduct thorough security assessments of all third-party vendors.
    * **Code Audits:**  Where possible, request access to the source code of critical libraries.
    * **Dependency Tracking:** Maintain a detailed inventory of all third-party dependencies.
* **Leverage Akamai’s Solutions:**  Continue to utilize and expand the deployment of Akamai’s Client-Side Protection & Compliance solution to provide real-time protection against supply chain attacks.
* **Continuous Monitoring:** Enhance our monitoring capabilities to proactively identify and respond to emerging threats.

**Conclusion:**

The Polyfill.io attack serves as a stark reminder of the evolving threat landscape and the importance of a layered security approach.  Akamai is committed to providing our customers with the tools and expertise they need to mitigate these risks and maintain the security and trust of their users. We are actively collaborating with customers to address the impact of this attack and prevent future incidents.
