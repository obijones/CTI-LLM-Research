This report details a significant cybersecurity incident involving the 3CX PBX software provider, highlighting a sophisticated supply chain attack orchestrated by a North Korean (likely Lazarus Group) actor. This incident underscores the critical importance of robust supply chain security measures and demonstrates the evolving tactics employed by state-sponsored actors.

**Key Findings:**

* **Targeted Platform:** While initially focused on Windows, the attack successfully targeted 3CX’s macOS customer base, highlighting the need for comprehensive security assessments across all operating systems.

    * **March 29, 2023:** CrowdStrike confirmed the supply chain attack, identifying malicious activity originating from a legitimate 3CXDesktopApp binary.

    * **March 29, 2023:** SentinelOne published a preliminary report.
* **Root Cause:** The initial compromise stemmed from an employee installing Trading Technologies’ software on a personal computer, allowing the attacker (UNC4736) to establish persistence.
* **Impact:** The attack resulted in widespread infection of 3CX customers, potentially exposing sensitive data and disrupting business operations.
