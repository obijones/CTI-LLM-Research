**Executive Threat Intelligence Report – Ransomware Risk Assessment**

**1. Executive Summary**

LockBit 3.0 ransomware affiliates are actively targeting organizations across multiple critical infrastructure sectors, including financial services, and pose a significant and immediate risk to our operations. This threat actor’s demonstrated ability to compromise systems and demand ransom payments represents a direct threat to our ability to conduct business, protect sensitive data, and maintain regulatory compliance. We must take immediate action to mitigate this risk.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

A successful LockBit 3.0 attack could result in substantial operational disruption, potentially halting critical financial transactions and impacting our ability to serve customers. The potential for data loss, including customer information and financial records, carries significant financial consequences due to regulatory fines (such as those imposed by GDPR or CCPA), legal liabilities, and the cost of remediation. Furthermore, a successful ransomware attack would severely damage our reputation and erode customer trust, leading to potential revenue loss and long-term brand damage.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The threat actor’s activity targets organizations utilizing Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software, which is a core component of our infrastructure. This exposure directly impacts our ability to process transactions, manage network security, and maintain operational resilience. The potential for compromise extends across multiple business functions, including payment processing, customer service, and internal communications.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Immediately isolate** all systems utilizing Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway software to prevent potential compromise and limit the scope of a potential attack. This action directly reduces the risk of further system infiltration and data exfiltration.
*   **Conduct a thorough assessment** of all systems for evidence of compromise, focusing on identifying any unauthorized access or malicious activity. This assessment will allow us to determine the extent of the potential impact and prioritize remediation efforts.
*   **Reimage** any systems identified as potentially compromised, ensuring the removal of any malicious software or backdoors. This action eliminates the source of the threat and prevents further exploitation.
*   **Create new account credentials** for all administrative accounts, limiting the potential damage if an account is compromised. This strengthens our security posture and reduces the risk of unauthorized access.
*   **Report the compromise** to the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) and CISA via the agency’s Incident Reporting System or its 24/7 Operations Center at Report@cisa.gov or (888) 282-0870. This ensures we receive timely information and support from relevant authorities and security experts.
