**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

Currently, the vulnerability is confirmed to affect SonicWall seventh-generation and newer firewalls that utilize SSL VPN access. The attack chain begins with compromising the firewall itself, followed by a range of tactics including reconnaissance, lateral movement within the network, and credential theft. We are observing a pattern of immediate privilege escalation, suggesting a targeted approach by the attackers. The rapid spread of this threat highlights the importance of immediate containment measures.

**4. Recommended Actions**

Given the urgency of this situation, we must act decisively and immediately. The following steps are critical:

*   **Immediate Firewall Lockdown:** Disable SSL VPN access on all SonicWall firewalls until a definitive patch and guidance are released. This is the most effective immediate defense.

*   **Account Audits:** Conduct a thorough review of all user accounts, particularly those with administrative privileges, and enforce the principle of least privilege.

*   **Continuous Monitoring:** Increase monitoring efforts to detect any suspicious activity, focusing on lateral movement, credential access, and unusual network traffic.
