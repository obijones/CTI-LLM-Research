**Executive Threat Intelligence Report: SonicWall Zero-Day Vulnerability**

**To:** Senior Business Leadership
**From:** [Your Name/Cybersecurity Analyst Team]
**Date:** October 26, 2026
**Subject:** Critical Threat – Active Exploitation of SonicWall VPN Vulnerability

**Executive Summary:**

We’ve identified a highly active and critical threat targeting SonicWall seventh-generation firewall appliances. A zero-day vulnerability is being exploited, leading to widespread incidents, including ransomware deployment (likely Akira) and significant network compromise. Immediate action is required to mitigate the risk to our organization. This report outlines the key findings and recommends urgent steps.

**Key Findings:**

*   **Active Exploitation:** A zero-day vulnerability exists within SonicWall VPN firmware versions 7.2.0-7015 and earlier. Threat actors are actively exploiting this vulnerability.
*   **Attack Vector:** The initial breach typically occurs through the SonicWall appliance itself, followed by a rapid sequence of post-exploitation activities.
*   **Tactics Observed:** We’ve observed the following tactics being employed by the attackers:
    *   **Privilege Escalation:** Leveraging overprivileged LDAP or service accounts (e.g., “sonicwall,” “LDAPAdmin”) to gain administrative access.
    *   **Lateral Movement:** Utilizing techniques to move within the network after initial compromise.
    *   **Detection Evasion:** Attempts to evade security tools, including the use of Windows drivers (rwdrv.sys and hlpdrv.sys) – a common BYOVD attack technique.
    *   **Malicious Activity:**  Specifically, we’ve seen attempts to delete Volume Shadow Copies via WMI (using PowerShell) and resetting local user account passwords for accounts with SSL VPN access.
*   **Confirmed Incidents:** As of August 6, 2026, at least eight additional organizations have been impacted, totaling over 28 confirmed incidents linked to this activity. One incident involved intrusion and attempted deletion of Volume Shadow Copies.
*   **Indicators of Compromise (IOCs):** The following IP addresses have been associated with the threat actors: 45.86.208.240, 77.247.126.239, 104.238.205.105, 104.238.220.216, 181.215.182.64.

**Immediate Recommendations:**

1.  **Disable SonicWall VPN:** Immediately disable SonicWall VPN access on all SonicWall appliances. This is the highest priority mitigation step.
2.  **Network Segmentation:** Segment the network to prevent a compromised SonicWall appliance from providing immediate access to critical servers (domain controllers, etc.).
3.  **Account Audits:** Conduct a thorough audit of all service accounts, particularly those associated with the SonicWall device or LDAP integration. Ensure accounts adhere to the principle of least privilege.
4.  **Credential Rotation:** Immediately rotate credentials – both local user account and LDAP account passwords – used for Active Directory integration, as recommended by Huntress.
5.  **Monitor for IOCs:** Continuously monitor your environment for the listed IOCs and any other suspicious activity.
6.  **External Recon Tool:** Utilize the Huntress external recon tool ([https://.huntress.io/account/external_recon](https://.huntress.io/account/external_recon)) to scan for the default port (4433) on your network.

**Severity:** Critical – Requires immediate and sustained attention.

**Next Steps:**

Our team is continuing to monitor this threat and will provide further updates as new information becomes available. We will be conducting a full forensic analysis of any impacted systems.
