**Executive Summary:**

We’ve identified a critical, actively exploited zero-day vulnerability (CVE-2024-3400) targeting PAN-OS devices, specifically versions 10.2.4-h16, 11.0 and 11.1. This vulnerability allows for unauthenticated remote code execution with root privileges, posing a significant risk to our network security. Kroll observations indicate widespread exploitation, and we are actively monitoring the situation and coordinating with Palo Alto Networks and Kroll Responder MDR. Immediate action is required to mitigate this threat.

**Key Findings:**

*   **Vulnerability Details:** CVE-2024-3400 is a command injection vulnerability within the GlobalProtect feature of PAN-OS. The vulnerability stems from improper sanitization of SessionIDs, allowing attackers to execute arbitrary commands.
*   **Active Exploitation:** Kroll has observed multiple geographically distributed actors actively exploiting this vulnerability.  Crucially, we have *not* yet detected successful exploitation on patched devices, which is a positive indicator, but the volume of activity suggests a sustained and significant threat.
*   **Affected PAN-OS Versions:** The vulnerability impacts PAN-OS 10.2.4-h16, 11.0 (including 11.0.4-h1, 11.0.3-h10, 11.0.2-h4, and 11.0.1-h4), and 11.1 (including 11.1.2-h3, 11.1.1-h1, and 11.1.0-h3).
*   **Immediate Mitigation:** Palo Alto Networks has released hotfixes addressing this vulnerability. We strongly recommend immediate patching of all affected devices.  Specific hotfix ETAs are detailed in the attached document.
*   **Threat Intelligence:** Kroll is tracking the exploitation methods, including the use of the Gorilla framework, and has added associated IOCs to our detection database.

**Recommendations:**

1.  **Immediate Patching:** Deploy the latest PAN-OS hotfixes (as outlined in Context 7) to all affected devices *immediately*. This is the highest priority action.
