**Executive Threat Intelligence Report: Polyfill.io Supply Chain Attack**

**To:** Senior Business Leadership
**From:** [Your Name/Cybersecurity Analyst]
**Date:** October 26, 2023
**Subject:** Urgent: Critical Vulnerability Exploited via Third-Party JavaScript – Mitigating Supply Chain Risks

**Executive Summary:**

Akamai has identified and is actively responding to a significant cyberattack originating from the compromise of polyfill.io, a widely used third-party JavaScript service. This attack exploited vulnerabilities within web applications relying on polyfills to deliver malicious code, impacting over 100,000 websites and potentially exposing customer data.  The attack highlights critical risks associated with third-party dependencies and underscores the importance of robust client-side protection and proactive threat monitoring.

**Key Findings:**

* **Attack Vector:** The attack leveraged compromised polyfill.io resources injected into web applications. Polyfills, designed to provide compatibility for older browsers, were exploited to deliver malicious JavaScript.
* **Scale of Impact:**  Over 40 Akamai customers were affected, with some experiencing tens of thousands of impacted pages daily. This demonstrates the potential for widespread damage from similar supply chain attacks.
* **Detection & Mitigation:** Akamai’s Client-Side Protection & Compliance solution, operating *in-browser*, was instrumental in detecting the malicious JavaScript execution and immediately preventing attack execution. This real-time mitigation capability is a key differentiator.
* **Root Cause:** The vulnerability stemmed from reliance on a compromised third-party service and a lack of granular control over the JavaScript resources being loaded by web applications.
* **Specific Tactics:** The attack utilized techniques like sampling (targeting mobile devices based on request headers) and referrer verification to focus malicious code delivery.

**Implications & Recommendations:**

1. **Increased Vigilance:**  This incident reinforces the need for heightened awareness of supply chain risks.  We must continuously assess the security posture of all third-party vendors and services.
2. **Strengthened Client-Side Protection:**  Continue to invest in and leverage solutions like Akamai’s Client-Side Protection & Compliance, which provide real-time monitoring and mitigation of client-side threats.
3. **Vendor Risk Management:**  Implement a more rigorous vendor risk management program, including enhanced security assessments and contractual requirements for third-party providers.
4. **Code Review & Security Scanning:**  Conduct thorough code reviews and automated security scanning of web applications to identify and eliminate vulnerabilities related to third-party JavaScript dependencies.

**Moving Forward:**

Akamai is actively collaborating with affected customers and providing a security bulletin to address the situation. We are continuously monitoring the threat landscape and refining our detection and mitigation capabilities.  We will provide further updates as the situation evolves.
