* **Vulnerability Highlighted:** This incident underscores the inherent risks associated with relying on third-party services, particularly those providing core functionality like polyfills. The attack originated from both third-party developers and user browser extensions.
* **Agility is Key:** The speed of response was critical. The ability to quickly detect and block the attack demonstrates the value of real-time monitoring and automated mitigation strategies.

**Strategic Implications & Recommendations:**

1. **Enhanced Third-Party Risk Management:** We must immediately strengthen our processes for vetting and monitoring third-party JavaScript resources. This includes:
