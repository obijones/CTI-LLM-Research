**Executive Threat Intelligence Report – Supply Chain Attack**

**1. Executive Summary**

A significant cyberattack involving compromised third-party resources, specifically polyfills, has impacted over 100,000 websites globally. This attack represents a critical risk to our organization’s operations, potentially leading to user data compromise and reputational damage. Immediate action is required to mitigate the risk of malicious code injection and disruption to online services.

**2. Potential Business Impact**

This supply chain attack poses a substantial threat to our business. A successful compromise could result in significant operational disruption, impacting our ability to deliver online services to our customers. Furthermore, the potential for user data exposure raises serious concerns regarding regulatory compliance (specifically, CJIS controls) and could trigger substantial reputational damage, eroding customer trust and potentially leading to financial losses.

**3. Affected Systems / Scope**

The attack targets websites reliant on third-party resources, including polyfills – pieces of code that enable modern website functionality on older browsers. This means that any website utilizing these compromised polyfills is vulnerable. The scale of the impact is considerable, with over 40 customers utilizing polyfill.io, experiencing tens of thousands of impacted pages daily. This exposes a wide range of our customers’ online operations, particularly those relying on web applications and modern website functionality.

**4. Recommended Actions**

*   **Immediately investigate all customer websites utilizing polyfill.io:** This proactive step will identify the extent of the compromise and allow for rapid containment measures.
*   **Implement Script Behavior Policies:** Activate policies designed to block suspicious communication, preventing the malicious code from executing and disrupting website functionality.
*   **Monitor JavaScript Resource Execution:** Enhance real-time monitoring of JavaScript resources across all web applications to detect anomalous behavior and identify potential future threats.
*   **Collaborate with Akamai Client-Side Protection & Compliance:** Leverage Akamai’s expertise in client-side threat detection and mitigation to rapidly respond to and contain the attack.
*   **Notify Affected Customers:** Promptly inform customers who utilize polyfill.io about the attack and provide guidance on mitigating the risk, ensuring transparency and minimizing potential disruption.
