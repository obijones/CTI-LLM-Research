**Subject:** Urgent: Active Exploitation of Cisco IOS XE WebUI Vulnerabilities – Immediate Mitigation Required

**Executive Summary:**

We are reporting an active and concerning threat landscape centered around vulnerabilities within Cisco IOS XE software, specifically the WebUI (Web Management User Interface).  A sophisticated actor is actively exploiting these vulnerabilities – CVE-2023-20198 and CVE-2023-20273 – to gain unauthorized administrative access to vulnerable devices.  This activity was initially observed as early as September 18th, 2023, and has escalated significantly following the publication of Proof-of-Concept (POC) code.  The current situation demands immediate action to minimize potential damage.

**Key Findings & Analysis:**

* **Active Exploitation:** Cisco Talos Intelligence is tracking significant exploitation attempts against vulnerable devices. The actor is leveraging publicly available POC code, indicating a broader potential impact.
* **“BadCandy” Web Shell:** The attacker is utilizing a Lua-based web shell named “BadCandy” to achieve persistent access. This shell is used for reconnaissance, log deletion, and user manipulation.
* **Tactics Observed:** The threat actor demonstrated advanced tactics, including:
    * **Initial Reconnaissance:** Gathering information about devices using commands like `clear logging` and user manipulation.
    * **Evidence Removal:**  Deleting logs and removing user accounts (e.g., "cisco_tac_admin") to obscure their activities – a clear indicator of a deliberate effort to cover their tracks.
    * **Implant Deployment:** Establishing persistent access via deployment of the “BadCandy” web shell, suggesting a longer-term operational objective.
* **Timeline of Activity:** The initial suspicious activity was detected as early as September 28th, 2023, with increased exploitation attempts observed following the release of the POCs on October 30th and 31st.
* **Alignment with Guidance:** This activity aligns with recommendations from both Cisco and the U.S. government (CISA Directive 23-02) regarding mitigating risks associated with internet-exposed management interfaces.

**Immediate Recommendations:**

1. **Immediate Patching:**  Prioritize patching all affected Cisco IOS XE devices *immediately*.  This is the highest priority action.
2. **Network Segmentation:** Implement network segmentation to limit the potential impact of a compromised device.
3. **Enhanced Monitoring:** Increase monitoring of network traffic and device logs for suspicious activity, specifically looking for the “BadCandy” web shell or related commands.
4. **Vulnerability Scanning:** Conduct thorough vulnerability scans to identify any remaining vulnerable devices.

**Severity Assessment:**

* **Critical:** The vulnerabilities allow for complete system compromise, potentially leading to data breaches, service disruption, and further exploitation.
* **Ongoing Threat:** The active exploitation and the availability of POC code represent a significant and sustained threat.
