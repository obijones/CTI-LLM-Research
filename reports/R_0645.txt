This vulnerability affects SonicWall seventh-generation and newer firewalls that utilize SSL VPN functionality. Specifically, we need to identify all SonicWall devices within our network infrastructure and any systems connected through those firewalls. A thorough review of our network architecture and connected devices is paramount.
